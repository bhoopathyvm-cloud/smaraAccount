# credit-card-household-flow

## Purpose

Capability for credit card household flow.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Credit Card Household Flow
Credit card accounts SHALL be identifiable in the UI. Recording spent SHALL offer paid from card versus paid from bank. Paying the card SHALL be offered as a labeled moved-money action from bank to card.

#### Scenario: Paid from card
- **WHEN the user records spent paid from card**
- **THEN** the expense posts against the selected credit card account
